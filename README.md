# Clothing_Store_Data_Analysis

Clothing Store Annual Report
Overview
This repository contains data analytics assets and interactive dashboard visuals for the annual performance of an online clothing store. The project includes raw sales data, a cleaned dataset with engineered features, and key business insights derived from detailed exploratory analysis.

Data Sources :
 Raw Data: Excel file with order-level details, including order ID, customer, gender, age, purchase date, sales channel, product details, and sales amount.

Cleaned Data:   Pre-processed version with deduplicated records and engineered columns (Age Category, Month, etc.) for analysis.

Dashboard Insights :

1) Sales Trends: Visualization tracking monthly sales and order volumes.

2) Customer Demographics: Analysis by gender and age group.

3) Order Fulfillment: Pie chart showing delivery rate and share of returns, cancellations, and refunds.

4) Regional Sales: Top-performing states by sales amount.

5) Channel Performance: Share of orders across platforms like Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, and others.



Addition of key analytic columns:

1) Age_Category (Adult, Young, Senior)

2) Month (from Date field)

3) Consistent formatting for gender, products, and status


Key Business Insights :
1) Women drive the majority of sales across channels.

2) Amazon is the leading sales channel, followed by Myntra and Flipkart.

3) Maharashtra and Karnataka are the top-performing states.

4) Most orders are delivered successfully, with minimal returns and cancellations.

5) Adults are the main buyer demographic.
